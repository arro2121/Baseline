Get Cosmo Sports on your phone

Live scores, predictions you can check, and free Cosmic cards. It installs straight from your browser: free, no App Store, and it opens full screen from its own icon.

Open Cosmo Sports

iPhone and iPad

  1. 1Open cosmosports.app in Safari
  2. 2Tap the Share button at the bottom
  3. 3Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen
  4. 4Tap Add. Done.

In Chrome on iPhone, Share is at the top right. Opened from Instagram, TikTok or Snapchat? Tap ••• and choose Open in Safari first.

Android

  1. 1Open cosmosports.app in Chrome
  2. 2Tap ⋮ at the top right
  3. 3Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen)
  4. 4Tap Install. Done.

Opened from another app? Tap ⋮ and choose Open in Chrome first.

On a computer? Scan this with your phone

It opens cosmosports.app. Then follow the steps for your phone.
