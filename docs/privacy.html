Baseline privacy policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Baseline is a free, non-commercial tennis prediction app. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking or analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your settings are stored only in your browser or app on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time in Settings > Your data > Reset, or by removing the app.

Services we use

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages, the live service runs on Cloudflare Workers, live scores come from API-Tennis and ESPN, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. These providers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP addresses) under their own privacy policies.

Children

Baseline includes sports betting tools and is intended for adults of legal gambling age where they live.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
