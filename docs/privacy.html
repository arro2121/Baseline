Cosmo Sports privacy policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Cosmo Sports is a free sports app. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking or analytics. We don't collect, sell or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, the teams you follow and your tennis watchlist are stored only in your browser or app on your device. They are never sent to us. You can erase them at any time in Settings > Your data > Reset, or by removing the app.

Notifications

If you turn on tennis alerts, your browser gives the app a push address that is stored by our live service (on Cloudflare) together with the players you chose, so it can alert you. Turning alerts off deletes it.

Services the app talks to

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Scores, play-by-play, box scores, standings, schedules, rosters, news, highlight clips, player photos and team logos are loaded from ESPN; tennis live scores come from API-Tennis through our live service on Cloudflare Workers; fonts load from Google Fonts. These providers receive standard technical information when your device contacts them (such as your IP address) and handle it under their own privacy policies. Articles and videos you open on espn.com are covered by ESPN's policies.

Children

Cosmo Sports shows betting odds and betting tools and is intended for adults. It isn't directed to children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
