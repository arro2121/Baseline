Cosmo Sports

Every game, every league, one app.

Cosmo Sports brings live scores, play-by-play with animated replays, box scores, standings, team pages, news and data-driven predictions for tennis, the NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL and the Premier League together in one place, with alerts for your teams, Ask Cosmo (an AI sports companion), and daily picks and games.

Where the data comes from

Baseball comes from MLB's official Stats API, including MLB's own highlight clips. Other live scores, play-by-play, box scores, standings, schedules, rosters, news, highlight clips and team logos come from ESPN's public sports data, with the NHL's official API as a backup for hockey; ESPN's articles and videos open or play from ESPN. Tennis results and ratings are built from Jeff Sackmann's Tennis Abstract data and API-Tennis; NFL history from nflverse; Premier League history from openfootball.

Predictions

Win chances come from Cosmo Sports' own rating models and are estimates, not guarantees. Betting lines are shown for information only. If you bet, bet only what you can afford to lose; in the US, call 1-800-GAMBLER for help.

Not affiliated

How Cosmo Sports works

The prediction

Every player has an Elo rating: a number that goes up when they win and down when they lose, by more when the result is a surprise. Cosmo Sports keeps one overall rating and one for each surface, built from every tour-level, Challenger, WTA 125, ITF and qualifying match since 2012.

A model then combines 20 factors, including those ratings, serve and return, head-to-head, momentum, rest, workload, age, experience, height, home crowd and the Grand Slam stage. Each factor's weight was learned from 2015–2023 matches and tested on 2024–26 matches the model never saw, where it picks about two-thirds of winners.

Reading the numbers

Win chance is the model's estimate, not a certainty: a 70% favorite still loses 3 times in 10. Fair price is the betting price that would exactly match that chance.

Betting terms

Bookmaker's cut: the margin built into the odds; Cosmo Sports removes it to find the market's true view. Edge: how much higher the model's chance is than the market's. ¼ Kelly: a cautious stake size based on your edge and bankroll. Closing line value: whether the odds moved your way before the match started, the most reliable early sign of a real edge.

Live games and play-by-play

In the installed app, the Live and Games sections follow matches as they happen, with every play (or, in tennis, every point) arriving within seconds. Live tennis win chances come from a point-by-point model of scoring that starts at the pre-match prediction.

Privacy

Your settings are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, ads or tracking.

Please bet responsibly

Predictions are estimates. Only bet what you can afford to lose, and if gambling stops being fun, contact a support service such as 1-800-GAMBLER (US) or BeGambleAware (UK).
