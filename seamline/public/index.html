        Measurements

        Use a soft tape measure. These decide the size, so a helper makes them more accurate.

        How to measure
        • Chest: around the fullest part, under the arms, tape level.
        • Waist: around where your pants usually sit.
        • Hips: around the fullest part of your seat, feet together.
        • Shoulders: across your back from one shoulder tip to the other.
        • Inseam: from your crotch to the floor, barefoot.
        • Arm: from the shoulder tip down to the wrist bone, arm relaxed.

        Clothes that fit you well

        Lay a top and a pair of pants you love flat on a table and measure them. This is the most accurate way to size, because it records how you like things to fit.
