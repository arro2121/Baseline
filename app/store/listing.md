## Description [4000]
Cosmo Sports is a sports card game. Collect cards of real NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, Premier League, tennis and college teams and players, then put them to work: build a lineup and it plays a whole game, play by play.

PLAY YOUR CARDS
- Pick a sport and a lineup of up to five of your cards
- Your lineup plays a full simulated game: nine innings, four quarters, three periods with overtime and a shootout, ninety minutes with penalties, or a tennis match
- Watch every play as it happens, or skip to the final
- Every card gets a box score line and a grade, and the best one is the player of the game
- Play the AI any time (its lineup is rated to match yours, so it's a fair 50/50), or challenge real people by name

CLIMB THE RANKINGS
- A game rating that starts at 1,000 and rises as you beat other players
- Game rankings, monthly seasons with rewards, and weekly winners

COLLECT
- Open packs of limited-edition cards in seven rarities, from Jupiter (1 of 1,000) to the Sun: only one of each will ever exist
- Rarer, higher-level and better real players rate higher in games
- Cards level up when the real player has a big game
- All-Time legends, Iconic Moments and two-player Binary Stars
- Trade with other collectors, run auctions, buy on the Market or sell back to the shop

BET ON REAL GAMES
- Put free Cosmic Coins on real games and player props at real prices, or build a parlay

YOUR ACCOUNT
- Sign in with a passkey: Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. No password and no email.
- Your coins and cards follow you to every device
- Delete your account any time in Settings

Cosmic Coins are play money. They can't be bought, and they can't be cashed out. Cards have no cash value. The simulated games are fictional. Cosmo Sports is for entertainment and doesn't offer real-money gambling. For ages 18 and over.

## App Review information (paste into "Notes")
The app needs a free account. Anyone can create one in about ten seconds, and no demo account is needed: on the Create account tab type any name, tick the 18+ box, tap Create account, then confirm with Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode. This creates a passkey, which works with the simulator's enrolled Face ID too. To see the main feature: open a free pack on Packs, then Play (bottom bar), pick the sport of your cards, Play AI, choose your cards and tap Play the game.

- Cosmic uses play money only. Cosmic Coins can't be bought with real money or exchanged for anything outside the app, and cards have no cash value. The odds for every card pack are shown on the Packs tab before opening.
- Account deletion: Settings (gear icon, top right) > Account > Delete account, or the Delete account link at the bottom of the Cosmic page.
- Leaderboard names are public (user-generated content). Offensive names are refused at sign-up. Any player's name can be tapped to **Report name** or **Block** (on the leaderboard, Market and live feed). Reports go to the developer's in-app inbox, where each one is reviewed and the name replaced if it breaks the rules. The Terms of Use (linked on the sign-in screen) prohibit objectionable content and abusive users.
- Simulated games are fictional: they're played out by the app from the cards' ratings, not predictions of real games.
- Native features: passkey sign-in (Associated Domains), haptic feedback, the native share sheet, links to cosmosports.app opening in the app, the App Store rating prompt, and an offline screen.
